## ❓ PART 14: OPEN QUESTIONS & CONCERNS

**Q14.1**: What are your biggest concerns about UI/UX?
Self-guiding gameplay, allowing a user to naturally understand the best next step to progress and continue in the game. Keeping a user engaged but not overwhelmed.

_______________

**Q14.2**: What is most important to get right?
The integration of educational content during gameplay, allowing users to understand the why behind the mechanics, empowering experimentation opposed to rigid repetion and blind guessing.

_______________

**Q14.3**: What are you most excited about?
The approachable and conscious breakdown of artificial intelligence concepts into understandable and relatable decision-making and problem-solving tools. My aim is to disarm the fear factor of AI and machine learning and expose players to a controlled environment where anyone can gain an understanding of what AI really is and what it really does and how it really works. I am of the opinion that fear is driven by lack of understanding, knowledge is power over that fear. Project Mission: to empower anyone to solve problems using technology.

_______________

**Q14.4**: Any features you're considering but unsure about?
Graphics complexity, social features.

_______________

**Q14.5**: What makes this project unique/special?
Projects showcasing machine learning concepts using game mechanics are nothing new. This game will focus on the simplicity of the learning experience and relatability of the problem solving approach. Instead of bringing users into the machine learning realm and making them select machine learning concepts, we can illustrate options as more relatable and intuitive concepts.

_______________

**Q14.6**: What should we absolutely avoid?
Conceptual copycating and over complexity of the technical descriptions. 

_______________

**Q14.7**: Any competitors or similar products to learn from?
AI Learns to Drive. The concept is nearly identical, training a vehicle to race, competing in leaderboards, and earning upgrades. The game is Steam desktop restricted though and focuses on concepts at a medium level where machine learning basics are required to truly understand some aspects of the game.

_______________

**Q14.8**: What would make this a "must-use" product for your target audience?
Accessibility and ease of play. The game should fall into the "addicting" semi-idler game category, where users can easily jump in and out of the game, but are motivated to keep playing and improving their models. 

_______________

**Q14.9**: What metrics define success for you?
- User engagement (daily active users, session length)
- Educational achievement (completion of tutorials, understanding of concepts, learning based unlocks)
- Competitive landscape (leaderboard activity, tournament participation)

_______________

**Q14.10**: Any other requirements, preferences, or context we should know?
This project also acts as a portfolio piece, aimed at showcasing my machine learning understanding and my ability to illustrate technology concepts in an approachable and un-intimidating way. The project is also a passion project, driven by my desire to empower others to understand and use technology to solve problems. There are many post-MVP directions this could go, for example, if the educational component is what is most important, the racing mechanic may shift. Our dot may be able to face other challenges, further allowing users to apply what they learn in dot car derby to the dots next challenge. The game mechanics and educational content should be designed with this potential future direction in mind, allowing for flexibility and adaptability as we learn more about our users and their needs. i.e. the educational content should be agnostic to the racing aspect and simplify the ML concepts themselves, regardless of the target task, further showcasing the flexibility of technology and algorithmic thinking as a solution path.
